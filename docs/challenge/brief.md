# Challenge brief: Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/junior-astronaut-mission-trainer/
Difficulty: Intermediate, Beginner/Youth. Subjects: Games, Planets & Moons, Software, Space Exploration, Sun.

## Objective (summarised)

Design and build an interactive game or app that lets kids ages 7 to 12 manage a lunar or Martian outpost, making real decisions about life support, radiation shielding, power budgeting, food production and communication delay, and experiencing how those choices play out over a simulated mission. Format is open: browser, mobile or desktop.

## Requirements checklist

| # | Requirement | Type | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Working prototype that clearly demonstrates the trade-off mechanic | Must | Open |
| R2 | Target ages 7 to 12, plain language, terms explained in context | Must | Open |
| R3 | Every decision has a real cost; no "safe" free choice | Must | Open |
| R4 | Choices play out over a simulated mission (time matters) | Must | Open |
| R5 | Player leaves understanding a real deep-space constraint | Must | Open |
| R6 | Ground in real NASA open data (DONKI, OSDR, Artemis/Mars surface data) | Encouraged | Open |
| R7 | Attribute data sources inside the tool | Encouraged | Open |
| R8 | Do not present static data as live | Must (honesty) | Open |
| R9 | Browser-playable, no download or account | Encouraged | Open |
| R10 | Two or three core resources, not five or six | Encouraged | Open |
| R11 | Flex to older siblings, parents, classrooms | Encouraged | Open |

## What stands out to judges

- Linking an in-game choice to real current data (for example shielding vs. live space weather).
- Replayability: events that vary between playthroughs, failure that teaches without punishing.
- A short explanation of which NASA datasets were used and why.

## Pitfalls named in the brief

- Visual polish substituting for mechanical accuracy.
- Too many simultaneous systems for young players.
- A choice with no cost.
