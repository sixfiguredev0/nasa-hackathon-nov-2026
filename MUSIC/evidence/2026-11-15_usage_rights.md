# Usage rights for the bundled game audio: checked 2026-11-15

Account: ElevenLabs **Creator** (paid, self-serve), active during all generations
(`account_get_usage`). All assets were generated by the account holder through the ElevenLabs
MCP connection. No reference audio, artist names, song titles or copyrighted material went
into any generation. One experiment wired our own title take into the music node's audio
input. It was rejected and is not shipped.

## Findings

**Sound effects and ambience** (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`)
- Terms §1(c)(ii): paid users "may use the Services for commercial purposes".
- Terms §4(c)(ii): "you retain all rights in and to your Output". §4(a): downloaded output may
  be used outside the Services.
- No SFX-specific restriction applies to bundling cues inside a game.
- **Status: permitted.**

**Music** (`eleven_music_v2_5`)
- Exhibit A, Creator: "All online and offline commercial use permitted, except film, TV, radio,
  and Studio Games". Attribution is not required. "For Individual Use Only" eligibility.
- §4.8(j): a Studio Game is a publicly available game, and "does not include an Indie Game".
- §4.8(c): an Indie Game has earned < US$500,000 since first commercial release, and its
  developer or publisher earned < US$1,000,000 from all games in the latest financial year.
  §4.7: the developer decides whether it qualifies. If the game stops qualifying, there are 60
  days to move to a plan that allows Studio Games, or the music must be removed.
- §2.1 / §3.3: prompts must not name artists or songs or imitate an artist. Our prompts only
  describe genre, instruments, tempo and mood. See `prompts/production_v2.md`.
- §4.2: Music Library creation is prohibited. The tracks are used only as this game's score.
- **Status: permitted for RED FRONTIER as an Indie Game** (a hackathon project with no revenue).

## Conditions the team must keep in mind

1. Revisit this if RED FRONTIER is commercialised beyond the Indie Game thresholds. The music
   would then need an Enterprise plan or replacement.
2. "For Individual Use Only" applies to who holds the plan. The generating account is an
   individual account. If a company or studio publishes the game, re-check eligibility.
3. Do not redistribute the cues as a standalone sound pack or music library.

No genuine usage-rights blocker for committing the assets to the team repository and shipping
them in this game. This is a plain reading of the published terms, not legal advice.
