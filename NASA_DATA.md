# NASA data and scientific integrity

## Official 2026 guidance
NASA Global Organizing Team [guidance (Nov 5, 2026)](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1827) permits pre-event challenge selection, resource review, topic research and team formation. It says not to start project work before the official event start; working ahead can lead to disqualification. For Global Awards, use at least one NASA data source included in the selected challenge's Resources. Cite all sources and list every data source, resource and tool used, including code, text and images. Obtain permission for copyrighted material; third-party material is allowed with appropriate attribution/permission.

Official [event hub](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) states Nov 14 at 09:00 local event time. Sofia's local listing says hacking begins 10:30; see SUBMISSION.md. If unsure whether preparation becomes project work, ask the Local Lead and wait for explicit guidance.

## Evidence standard
Prefer the challenge Resources and original provider documentation. Preserve units, coverage, resolution and quality flags. Document filters, conversions, aggregation, missing values, assumptions, version and date. Separate observed data from derived estimates, simulations and illustrative mock data. Avoid causal claims from correlation and claims of operational validation. State uncertainty and limitations plainly; have a teammate check important claims.
